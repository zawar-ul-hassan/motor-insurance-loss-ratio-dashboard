🚗 Insurance Claims & Premium Analysis Dashboard
A Power BI Project by Zawar

📌 Overview
I created this Insurance Claims & Premium Analysis dashboard to understand how claim patterns, premium structures, and vehicle usage types impact the financial health of an insurance company. The project tells the story of Horizon Shield Insurance — a company that entered the motor insurance market with ambition but struggled due to extremely high claim‑to‑premium ratios.

This dashboard brings together key metrics such as total policies, premium collected, total claims, loss ratios, average premium, average claim, and claim counts. Through structured data modeling, DAX calculations, and clean visual design, I transformed raw insurance data into a narrative that explains why the company faced continuous financial strain.

🎯 Key Features

Company‑Level KPIs: Total premium, total claims, claim‑to‑premium ratio, average premium, average claim, and policy count.

Vehicle Category Risk Analysis: Claim ratios for automobiles, trucks, trailers, tankers, pick‑ups, motorcycles, and more.

Premium Trends Over Time: Year‑wise premium distribution across usage types from 2014 to 2018.

Gender‑Based Claim Ratios: Comparison of claim‑to‑premium ratios for male vs female policyholders.

Average Premium vs Average Claim: Scatter plot showing risk concentration across vehicle types.

High‑Risk Vehicle Table: Detailed breakdown of makes, usage, premiums, claims, and extreme loss ratios.

Quarterly Loss Ratio Story: Visual timeline showing how the company only turned profitable in Q2 2018.

🛠️ What I Did

Modeled raw insurance data across multiple years

Built DAX measures for claim ratios, averages, and category‑wise performance

Designed a multi‑page dashboard for overview, details, and storytelling

Visualized high‑risk vehicle categories and usage types

Created a narrative explaining the company’s financial decline

🚀 Purpose

This project reflects my approach as a data analyst: using data to uncover hidden patterns, explain business outcomes, and tell a clear story. By analyzing premiums, claims, and loss ratios, this dashboard shows how risk mispricing can lead to long‑term financial instability.
